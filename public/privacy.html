SmartScan AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 8, 2026

SmartScan AI helps users scan documents, extract text, review key details, organize expenses, track payment due dates and export document information.

Information processed

In the current version, document images, extracted text and scan history are processed and stored locally on the user's device or browser storage. The app does not require an account.

Camera and files

Camera and file access are used only when the user chooses to scan or import a document. Users control which files are selected.

Data sharing

The current version does not sell user data and does not send scanned document contents to advertising networks. If cloud sync, analytics or third-party AI services are added later, this policy and store privacy disclosures will be updated before release.

Data deletion

Users can remove locally stored documents by clearing the app/browser storage. Future account or cloud features will provide appropriate deletion controls.

Accuracy

OCR and automated extraction can make mistakes. Verify important financial, legal, tax, medical or contractual information against the original document.

Contact

Support contact details will be finalized before public store submission.
