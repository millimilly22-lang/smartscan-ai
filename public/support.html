Need help using SmartScan AI?

Common help

OCR is inaccurate: Retake the scan on a flat surface with strong, even lighting and keep the full document inside the frame.

A total or date is not detected: Review the extracted text. Automatic detection is assistive and may miss unusual layouts.

Export: Open a document and choose Export PDF. Expense data can be exported from the Expenses page.
